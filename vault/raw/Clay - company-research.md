# Clay — Company Research

---

### Step 1: Company Snapshot & Business Model
- **What they do:** GTM data + agentic-automation platform — combines 1st/3rd-party data from **200+ providers (waterfall enrichment)**, AI research agents (**Claygent**), and orchestration/execution (sequencer, ads, CRM sync) so GTM teams automate prospecting, enrichment, scoring, and outreach.
- **Business Model:** Self-serve + enterprise SaaS, **credit-based usage pricing** (14-day Pro trial); one contract to buy data across 200+ vendors; partner/agency ecosystem + "GTM Engineer" certification/job-board flywheel.
- **Stage & Funding:** Founded 2017. $1.3B (Jan 2025) → Sequoia-led tender $1.5B (May 2025) → **$100M Series C at $3.1B (Aug 2025)** → tender at **$5B (Jan 2026)**. Investors: Sequoia, Meritech, Boldstart, BoxGroup, First Round.
- **Size & Location:** ~100+ (likely understated). HQ **NYC (Chelsea)**; global crew (Egypt, India, France, UK, Kenya, Lebanon, Canada).
- **Customers:** OpenAI, Anthropic, Intercom, Figma, Canva, Verkada, Vanta, Rippling, Notion, Google, HubSpot, Ramp, Stripe, Cursor, Perplexity, Mistral. Only GTM company on Forbes AI 50 2025.

### Step 2: Product Portfolio Deep-Dive
- **Core modules:** Data infrastructure (Audiences, Data Marketplace 200+ providers, Signals & Intent, Waterfall enrichment); Agents (Claygents; Agent plugin CLI/API; **Clay MCP**); Orchestration (Functions, AI formatting); Execution (Ads sync to LinkedIn/Meta/Google, Sequencer).
- **Use cases:** TAM sourcing, CRM enrichment, lead scoring/routing, automated inbound/outbound, ABM, PLG assist, territory planning, reverse ETL, rep prospecting/pre-call prep.
- **Customer outcomes:** Anthropic 3x enrichment; Intercom +140% outbound pipeline; Mistral TAM 2 months → 10 days; ElevenLabs +50% SQLs; Pendo reps at 200% quota.
- **Recent evolution:** Claygent AI research agents; **Clay MCP + Agent plugin CLI/API** (bring Clay into coding agents like Cursor); Signals real-time intent; TAM sourcing; Ads execution.

### Step 3: Competitive Landscape & Moat
- **Direct competitors:** ZoomInfo, Apollo.io, Clearbit/HubSpot Breeze, LeadIQ, Common Room, Koala.
- **Indirect:** In-house data/RevOps teams; SDR outsourcing; DIY Salesforce/Snowflake reverse-ETL.
- **Moat:** (1) Aggregator moat — one contract to 200+ vendors + waterfall = best coverage; (2) orchestration-layer positioning ("Salesforce for records, Snowflake for product data, Clay to turn it into action"); (3) **category-creation community moat** — Clay minted the "GTM Engineer" role, runs University, a Slack community, campus ambassadors, an experts marketplace, a GTM-Engineer job board, and the Sculpt conference; (4) marquee AI-lab customers (OpenAI, Anthropic).
- **Position:** Category-defining disruptor / emerging leader in the "GTM engineering" category it created.

### Step 4: Future Goals & Growth Opportunities
- **Strategic focus:** Become the orchestration layer / operating system for GTM; ride the agentic wave (Claygent, MCP, coding-agent installs).
- **Scale next:** Enterprise up-market (dedicated Enterprise + PE offerings, Enterprise Growth Strategist team), international (London/EMEA), partner/agency ecosystem, deeper agentic/dev tooling (API/CLI/MCP).
