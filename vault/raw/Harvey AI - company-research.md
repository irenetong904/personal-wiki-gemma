# Harvey AI — Company Research

---

### Step 1: Company Snapshot & Business Model
- **What they do:** Domain-specific ("professional-class") AI for legal & professional services — "the operating system for legal work." Agents + workflow tools that execute legal work end-to-end (contract analysis, due diligence, research, litigation, compliance, fund formation).
- **Business Model:** B2B enterprise SaaS — seat/enterprise subscriptions to law firms and corporate in-house teams, plus embedded "legal engineering" teams that build custom agents.
- **Stage & Funding:** Late-stage growth. **$200M at an $11B valuation (Mar 25, 2026)**, co-led by returning **GIC and Sequoia** (a16z, Coatue, Conviction, Elad Gil, Kleiner Perkins). **Total raised >$1B.** Additional strategic investment from **Goldman Sachs and J.P. Morgan (Jul 28, 2026).** Other backers: OpenAI Startup Fund, GV, EQT.
- **Size & Location:** HQ SF; offices NYC, Chicago, Dallas, London, Sydney, Singapore (+ remote). ~360 postings signal aggressive scale (many hundreds of employees).
- **Customers:** 2,400+ customers, 70+ countries, **75+ of the AmLaw 100**, 500+ in-house teams, 50 asset-management firms, 100,000+ lawyers. Logos: PwC UK, Deutsche Telekom, A&O Shearman, Reed Smith, KKR, Bridgewater, Comcast, P&G, Dentons; recent NBCUniversal, HSBC, DLA Piper.

### Step 2: Product Portfolio Deep-Dive
- **Core modules:** **Agents** (purpose-built agents; 25,000+ custom built via **Agent Builder**), **Vault** (secure bulk doc storage/analysis), **Knowledge** (legal/regulatory/tax research), **Shared Spaces**, **Command Center** (analytics/benchmarking for AI-transformation leaders), **Contract Intelligence**, **Harvey Mobile**, **Ecosystem** (integrations).
- **Use cases:** M&A due diligence, contract drafting/analysis, document review, litigation strategy, compliance, fund formation.
- **Recent evolution:** Shift from "assistant" to **agentic / long-horizon agents** (2025–26); launched Agent Builder, Shared Spaces, Customer Advisory Board (Mar 2026); **first certified AI agents in legal — AIUC-1** (Jul 30, 2026); expanded Microsoft collaboration (Jul 23, 2026); Harvey Academy. On Forbes AI 50 (2025 & 2026), Fast Company Most Innovative 2026, Time100.

### Step 3: Competitive Landscape & Moat
- **Competitors:** Legora, Robin AI, Spellbook (contract-focused), Thomson Reuters CoCounsel/Casetext, LexisNexis (Protégé); horizontal LLMs (OpenAI/Anthropic) used directly by firms.
- **Moat:** Deep enterprise trust (SOC2 II, ISO 27001/27701/42001, GDPR, AIUC-1); embedded legal-engineering teams → high switching costs; marquee logo density (majority of AmLaw 100); strategic capital + distribution (Sequoia, GIC, Goldman, JPM, Microsoft); proprietary legal benchmarks.
- **Position:** Clear category leader in legal AI by funding, valuation, and enterprise penetration.

### Step 4: Future Goals & Growth Opportunities
- **Strategic focus:** Scale the number of agents customers run; expand embedded legal-engineering globally; push agentic autonomy (long-horizon agents) + firm-wide "AI transformation" via Command Center.
- **Scale next:** Adjacent professional services (tax/audit/consulting — PwC/Adecco/Syngenta), mid-sized firms, international (60–70 countries), partner/ecosystem co-build model.
