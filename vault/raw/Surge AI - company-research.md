# Surge AI — Company Research

---

### Step 1: Company Snapshot & Business Model
- **What they do:** Human-data company for frontier AI — RLHF/data labeling, **RL environments, evaluations, and an elite expert workforce** that trains and grades the world's top LLMs. "Human intelligence for AGI."
- **Business Model:** B2B services + platform; bespoke human-data programs + **off-the-shelf ("OTS") frontier data and RL environments.** **Profitable from day one; entirely bootstrapped (no VC).**
- **Stage & Funding:** Founded **2020**. **Zero external funding.** Revenue **>$1B in 2024** (Forbes: $1.2B; The Information first reported), reportedly ~$1.4B run-rate 2025; fielding VC term sheets while staying independent (circulating valuation figures ~$15–30B are **unverified**).
- **Size & Location:** Lean — **~100–120 employees** at the $1B milestone (getlatka lists 121). HQ **San Francisco**; most roles remote.
- **Customers:** **Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, Meta, Microsoft.**

### Step 2: Product Portfolio Deep-Dive
- **Core products:** (1) Expert Workforce (credential-matched annotators via a talent-matching algorithm); (2) Off-the-Shelf frontier data & RL environments; (3) Evaluations/benchmarks ("beyond leaderboards"); (4) platform for scalable oversight.
- **Use cases:** RLHF, agentic/coding RL environments, complex-domain evals (law, HR, physics, finance, healthcare, coding), content moderation, multilingual data.
- **Recent evolution:** Emphasis on agentic behavior, coding RL environments, and evals; launched OTS data + RL-environment catalog and a research/benchmarks arm (2025). CEO publicly critiques LMArena/LMSYS "optimizing for the wrong objectives."

### Step 3: Competitive Landscape & Moat
- **Competitors:** **Scale AI** (Meta-backed, ~$29B), Mercor, Turing, Invisible Technologies; Appen/Labelbox (legacy).
- **Moat:** Early focus on complex, agentic, expert-level data (vs. commodity labeling); proprietary talent-matching → low annotator churn / high quality; extreme capital efficiency + profitability = pricing/independence leverage; deep frontier-lab relationships.
- **Position:** Quiet market leader by revenue in human data — larger than Scale by 2024 revenue, without raising a dollar.

### Step 4: Future Goals & Growth Opportunities
- **Strategic focus:** "Raise AGI with the richness of human intelligence" — scale RL environments + evaluations as models become agentic; keep independence/profitability.
- **Scale next:** Vertical domain data (finance, healthcare, legal), coding-agent RL envs, OTS productization, international recruitment of expert contributors.

### Step 5: Leadership Team (Targeted)
- **Edwin Chen — Founder & CEO.** Former ML engineer at Facebook, Google, Twitter; MIT (math/CS/linguistics). Highly public (Inc., Forbes, Lenny's Newsletter, 20VC) on quality-over-scale, lean org design, and correct eval objectives — **primary outreach contact.**
- *Unusually flat, private org; no public exec roster.*

### Step 6: Hiring Trends & Target Roles Deep-Dive
- **Macro trend:** ~50 open roles across Research/DS, Platform Eng, Field Engineering & Operations, AM/BD, Finance, Marketing — mostly **remote**. Signals broad scaling of the ops/GTM layer around a small core + finance maturation (Director FP&A, Product Finance).
- **Open ops/strategy/product roles (best fits):**
  - **Business Operations Manager** — bridge key accounts ↔ internal teams; owns RFPs, contract negotiation, PO/invoicing, growth-opportunity ID.
  - **Strategic Projects Lead** — optimize & scale data-collection/processing pipelines; data-quality metrics; **SQL/Python a plus.**
  - **Product Operations Manager** — own product-ops cycle, align product/eng/ops, define & track KPIs.
  - **Data Science, Growth**; **AI Programs Analyst (Finance Domain)**; **Business Analyst**; **Program Manager**; **Strategic Partnerships Manager**.
- **Note:** JDs list "MBA a plus." A **part-time consultant / research fellowship** path (remote) is a real in-semester door.
