# Sierra — Company Research

---

### Step 1: Company Snapshot & Business Model
- **What they do:** Enterprise conversational AI agents deployed across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice, and ChatGPT to resolve customer issues and drive revenue.
- **Business Model:** B2B SaaS with **outcome-based pricing** ("pay for a job well done" — pay for resolved outcomes, not seats).
- **Stage & Funding:** Founded 2023. $175M at $4.5B (Oct 2024) → raising **$950M led by Tiger Global & GV at >$15B post** (May 2026). Prior: Sequoia, Benchmark. **$100M ARR (Nov 2025) → $150M ARR (Feb 2026)**; 40%+ of the Fortune 50 as customers; agents handling "billions of interactions."
- **Size & Location:** HQ San Francisco; offices NY, Atlanta, London, Singapore, Tokyo, Paris, Madrid, Toronto. In-person culture; ~190 open roles.
- **Customers:** Rocket Mortgage, Gap, SiriusXM, SoftBank, Singtel, Vanguard, Santander, DIRECTV, ADT, Sonos, Ramp, SoFi, Discord, Nubank, Fanatics.

### Step 2: Product Portfolio Deep-Dive
- **Core modules:** **Agent Studio** (build/manage); **Ghostwriter** ("agent-building agent" — turns SOPs/transcripts/plain-English goals into a production agent); **Horizon** (long-horizon agents planning multi-step outcomes over days/months, with memory); **Context Engine**; **Insights/Explorer/Monitors/Experiments/Observability**; omnichannel **Channels**.
- **Use cases:** Service deflection, revenue growth, churn prevention, proactive next-best-action, claims/returns/activation, multilingual 24/7 support.
- **Recent evolution:** **Ghostwriter launched Apr 2026** ("agent as a service"); **Horizon** long-horizon agents; acquired YC-backed **Fragment (Apr 2026)**. Strong push into voice + "agents building agents."

### Step 3: Competitive Landscape & Moat
- **Direct competitors:** Decagon, Intercom (Fin), Parloa, Salesforce Agentforce, Ada, Cresta.
- **Indirect:** In-house LLM builds; incumbent CCaaS (Genesys, NICE, Zendesk AI).
- **Moat:** Bret Taylor's enterprise distribution/credibility; outcome-based pricing aligns vendor/buyer incentives (hard for incumbents to match); full agent lifecycle platform + Ghostwriter; deep compliance (SOC 2, ISO 27001/42001, HIPAA, FedRAMP, PCI DSS).
- **Position:** Front-running enterprise leader / category-definer in agentic CX; head-to-head with Decagon at the top of the market.

### Step 4: Future Goals & Growth Opportunities
- **Strategic focus:** Become the "global standard" for AI-powered CX; expand beyond customer-facing agents into internal/enterprise agents (Taylor's thesis that unused enterprise software gets replaced by agents).
- **Scale next:** International (9 offices, heavy multilingual hiring), regulated verticals (financial services, healthcare, insurance, telecom), voice, and a developer/platform ecosystem. >$1B war chest for land-grab + M&A.

### Step 5: Leadership Team (Targeted)

- **Clay Bavor — Co-founder.** 18 yrs at Google — led Google Labs, AR/VR (Project Starline), Lens, and Workspace product/design.
