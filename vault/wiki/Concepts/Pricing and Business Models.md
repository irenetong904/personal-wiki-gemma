# Pricing and Business Models

Several companies utilize usage-based or outcome-based pricing models for their AI/data services. Clay uses credit-based usage pricing, while ClickHouse Cloud employs usage/consumption-based pricing. Sierra specifically utilizes outcome-based pricing, charging for resolved customer issues.

## Details
- Clay utilizes credit-based usage pricing for its self-serve + enterprise SaaS model. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md|Clay - company-research.md]])
- ClickHouse Cloud operates on a usage/consumption-based pricing model, starting around ~$50/mo. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md|ClickHouse - company-research.md]])
- Sierra employs outcome-based pricing, meaning customers pay for resolved outcomes rather than seats. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md|Sierra - company-research.md]])
- Decagon's monetization is being refined, balancing SaaS subscriptions, usage-based pricing, and implementation effort. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
