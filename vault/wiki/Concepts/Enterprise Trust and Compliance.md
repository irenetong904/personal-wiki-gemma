# Enterprise Trust and Compliance

Enterprise trust is crucial because customer-facing AI failures can negatively impact brand trust and compliance. This trust is supported by features like security, guardrails, versioning, testing, human approval, and auditability.

## Details
- Enterprise trust is maintained through features such as security, guardrails, versioning, testing, human approval, and auditability. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
- Testing and QA/Simulations are central to enterprise trust, including auto-generated tests and CI/CD-style version testing. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
- Some platforms maintain deep compliance through certifications like SOC2 II, ISO 27001/27701/42001, GDPR, and AIUC-1. ([[raw/Harvey AI - company-research.md|Harvey AI - company-research.md]])
- Other platforms offer deep compliance with SOC 2, ISO 27001/42001, HIPAA, FedRAMP, and PCI DSS. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md|Sierra - company-research.md]])
