# AI Evaluation and Testing

AI evaluation and testing involves measuring model impact beyond simple data cleanliness, focusing on how data improves model capabilities. Specific approaches include using benchmarks, simulations, and dedicated observability tools for agent and LLM performance.

## Details
- David AI emphasizes measuring model impact and evaluations, differentiating itself from commodity data collection by focusing on whether data improves model capabilities. ([[raw/David AI - company-research.md|David AI - company-research.md]])
- Decagon's DuetBench, simulations, Watchtower, and Autopilot could create a potential category around ongoing agent QA, regression testing, and governance. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
- Testing and QA for agents includes validating behavior before and after updates, utilizing auto-generated tests, and CI/CD-style version testing. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
- Surge AI provides evaluations/benchmarks ('beyond leaderboards') and focuses on complex-domain evals for RLHF and agentic environments. ([[raw/Surge AI - company-research.md|Surge AI - company-research.md]])
- ClickHouse offers Langfuse for LLM observability and evaluations as part of its product suite. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md|ClickHouse - company-research.md]])
