# Harvey AI

Harvey AI provides domain-specific, professional-class AI, acting as an operating system for legal and professional services. The company operates on a B2B enterprise SaaS model, serving major law firms and corporate in-house teams globally. It has achieved significant funding and valuation, positioning itself as a category leader in legal AI.
