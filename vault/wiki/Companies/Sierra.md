# Sierra

Sierra provides enterprise conversational AI agents deployed across multiple channels to resolve customer issues and drive revenue. Their business model utilizes outcome-based pricing, aligning vendor incentives with achieved customer outcomes. The company is rapidly scaling, focusing on expanding into internal enterprise agents and regulated verticals.

## Key points
- Deploys conversational AI agents across chat, SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice, and ChatGPT. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
- Uses a B2B SaaS model with outcome-based pricing, paying for resolved outcomes. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
- Core modules include Agent Studio, Ghostwriter, and Horizon for long-horizon planning. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 2 Product Portfolio Deep-Dive|§ Step 2 Product Portfolio Deep-Dive]])
- The company's moat includes outcome-based pricing alignment and a full agent lifecycle platform. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 3 Competitive Landscape & Moat|§ Step 3 Competitive Landscape & Moat]])
- Strategic focus is to become the global standard for AI-powered CX and expand to internal agents. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 4 Future Goals & Growth Opportunities|§ Step 4 Future Goals & Growth Opportunities]])
- Bret Taylor is the Co-founder & CEO and Chair of OpenAI's board. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md#Step 5 Leadership Team (Targeted)|§ Step 5 Leadership Team (Targeted)]])
