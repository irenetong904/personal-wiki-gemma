# ClickHouse

ClickHouse is a leading open-source, column-oriented OLAP database used for real-time analytics at petabyte scale. It is expanding its focus to become a leading database for AI, offering a commercial cloud service alongside its open-source core. The company recently secured Series D funding and has a strong customer base including Meta and Anthropic.

- It is an open-source, column-oriented OLAP database for real-time analytics. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
