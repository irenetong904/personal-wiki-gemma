# Surge AI

Surge AI is a human-data company focused on providing intelligence for frontier AI, offering services like RLHF and data labeling. It operates on a B2B model, providing bespoke programs and off-the-shelf frontier data and RL environments, and is entirely bootstrapped.

- Provides RLHF/data labeling, RL environments, and an elite expert workforce to train LLMs. ([[raw/Surge AI - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
