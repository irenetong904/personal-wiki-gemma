# Decagon

Decagon is positioning itself as an enterprise "AI concierge" platform for customer experience, moving beyond being just a chatbot vendor. Its product thesis centers on creating continuously improving AI agents through a comprehensive operating loop involving iteration, governance, and measurable outcomes. The company is focused on winning large enterprise customers by building reliable agent infrastructure.

## Key points
- Decagon builds enterprise AI agents for customer experience across voice, chat, email, SMS, and other channels. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md#1. Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ 1. Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
- The company's product thesis is that AI agents should act like continuously improving teammates, not static SaaS configurations. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md#Product Thesis|§ Product Thesis]])
- Key product modules include AOPs, Duet, Duet Autopilot, and Watchtower for agent lifecycle management. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md#2. Product Portfolio Deep-Dive|§ 2. Product Portfolio Deep-Dive]])
- The company is focused on scaling from high-touch enterprise wins to a repeatable, globally deployable platform. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md#4. Future Goals & Growth Opportunities|§ 4. Future Goals & Growth Opportunities]])
