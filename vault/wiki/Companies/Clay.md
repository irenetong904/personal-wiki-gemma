# Clay

Clay is a GTM data and agentic-automation platform that allows GTM teams to automate prospecting, enrichment, scoring, and outreach. It combines data from over 200 providers with AI research agents like Claygent and orchestration tools. The company operates on a self-serve and enterprise SaaS model using credit-based usage pricing.

## Key points
- Combines 1st/3rd-party data from 200+ providers, Claygent agents, and orchestration tools. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
- Business Model is Self-serve + enterprise SaaS with credit-based usage pricing. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
- Core modules include Data infrastructure, Agents (Claygents), and Orchestration. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md#Step 2 Product Portfolio Deep-Dive|§ Step 2 Product Portfolio Deep-Dive]])
