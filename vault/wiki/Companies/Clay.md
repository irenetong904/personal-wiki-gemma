# Clay

Clay is a GTM data and agentic-automation platform that helps GTM teams automate prospecting, enrichment, scoring, and outreach. It combines data from over 200 providers with AI research agents like Claygent and orchestration tools. The company operates on a self-serve and enterprise SaaS model using credit-based usage pricing.

- Combines 1st/3rd-party data from 200+ providers, AI agents (Claygent), and orchestration. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md#Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model|§ Step 1 Company Snapshot & Business Model]])
