# David AI

David AI positions itself as the first audio data research lab, building the data layer required by frontier AI teams for speech, voice-agent, and multimodal AI. The company operates as a B2B audio data infrastructure provider, scaling a data factory rather than just selling static datasets. Its core process involves starting with a model capability hypothesis and productionizing the necessary audio data.
