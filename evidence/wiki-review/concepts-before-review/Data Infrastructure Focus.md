# Data Infrastructure Focus

The strategic focus across different companies involves building out specialized data infrastructure and operational layers to support AI and agentic applications. This includes becoming the default database layer for AI apps, scaling data factories, and establishing orchestration layers for Go-To-Market (GTM) functions.

## Details
- One strategic focus is to become the default database layer for AI/agentic apps and observability, integrating Langfuse and Postgres. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md|ClickHouse - company-research.md]])
- David AI's strategic focus is scaling its audio data production into a repeatable audio data factory to support more model capabilities and customers. ([[raw/David AI - company-research.md|David AI - company-research.md]])
- Clay's strategic focus is to become the orchestration layer or operating system for GTM, riding the agentic wave with Claygent and MCP. ([[raw/Clay - company-research.md|Clay - company-research.md]])
