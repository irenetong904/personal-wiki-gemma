# Business Model Innovation

Business models vary across companies, ranging from open-source core with commercial cloud offerings to outcome-based SaaS and bootstrapped B2B services. ClickHouse utilizes an OSS-to-cloud PLG model, while Sierra employs outcome-based pricing for its AI agents. Surge AI operates as a bootstrapped B2B service provider.

## Details
- ClickHouse uses an open-source core combined with commercial ClickHouse Cloud, which features usage/consumption-based pricing. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md|ClickHouse - company-research.md]])
- Sierra's business model is B2B SaaS that utilizes outcome-based pricing, meaning customers pay for resolved outcomes rather than seats. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md|Sierra - company-research.md]])
- Surge AI is entirely bootstrapped, operating as a B2B service and platform, and reported revenue exceeded $1B in 2024. ([[raw/Surge AI - company-research.md|Surge AI - company-research.md]])
- ClickHouse's offering spans real-time analytics, data warehousing, observability (ClickStack), and ML/GenAI. ([[raw/ClickHouse - company-research.md|ClickHouse - company-research.md]])
