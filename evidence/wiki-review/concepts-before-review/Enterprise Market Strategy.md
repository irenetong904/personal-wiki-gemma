# Enterprise Market Strategy

Enterprise market strategy involves establishing a leading position through deep integration, robust compliance, and specialized offerings. Companies achieve this by building moats through high switching costs, proprietary data, and strong enterprise trust.

## Details
- Decagon is positioned as an enterprise leader or category disruptor in AI customer experience agents, evidenced by its valuation and customer base. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
- Harvey AI operates on a B2B enterprise SaaS model, providing domain-specific AI for legal and professional services. ([[raw/Harvey AI - company-research.md|Harvey AI - company-research.md]])
- A key moat for Sierra involves outcome-based pricing and a full agent lifecycle platform, alongside deep compliance certifications. ([[raw/Sierra - company-research.md|Sierra - company-research.md]])
- Harvey AI's moat includes deep enterprise trust demonstrated by certifications like SOC2 II, ISO 27001/27701/42001, and GDPR. ([[raw/Harvey AI - company-research.md|Harvey AI - company-research.md]])
- Decagon's platform allows agents to connect to various enterprise systems like CRMs, help desks, and knowledge bases, which is critical for enterprise adoption. ([[raw/Decagon - company-research.md|Decagon - company-research.md]])
